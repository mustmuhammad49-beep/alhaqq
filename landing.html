Secular Critique Advanced
"The Quran permits men to beat their wives — the word daraba proves it"

Critics cite the word "daraba" in 4:34 as definitive proof that the Quran sanctions domestic violence, selecting the meaning "to strike" from a word that carries over a dozen meanings in Arabic.

Daraba has multiple meanings inside the Quran itself, including to separate and to set forth an example. 2:231 commands men not to hold wives in order to harm them. Allah does not command harm in one verse and forbid it in another — the separation reading is the one that stays consistent with His own Book.
